Research：我的研究關注「會學習的系統」如何從大腦與硬體的運算方式中汲取靈感，涵蓋世界模型（world models）、仿生學習（脈衝神經網路、predictive coding）、量子糾錯（QEC）解碼器的硬體實作，以及醫療訊號 AI。我喜歡把一個想法從模型一路做到晶片，在演算法與實際系統之間來回驗證。曾於 NSF HDR ML Challenge 2026 兩條賽道分別拿下全球第 2 與第 4 名。 Research: My research focuses on learning systems that draw on how the brain and hardware compute, spanning world models, bio-inspired learning (spiking neural networks and predictive coding), hardware decoders for quantum error correction, and medical signal AI. I enjoy carrying an idea from model all the way down to silicon, iterating between algorithms and working systems. I placed Global No. 2 and No. 4 on two tracks of the NSF HDR ML Challenge 2026.

Bio：我是國立清華大學電機工程學系的碩士生，在視覺科學實驗室（Vision Science Lab）跟隨孫民教授。大學畢業於清大電機系並修讀數據科學學程，期間於 Tedious Quantum Lab、NBME Lab、Data Lab 進行專題研究，並在 BiiC Lab 擔任研究助理，與台大醫院合作以心音估計心臟射血分率。 Bio: I am an M.S. student in Electrical Engineering at National Tsing Hua University (NTHU), advised by Prof. Min Sun at the Vision Science Lab. I received my B.S. in Electrical Engineering from NTHU with the Data Science Program, where I did research projects at the Tedious Quantum Lab, NBME Lab and Data Lab, and worked as a research assistant at BiiC Lab with NTU Hospital on estimating cardiac ejection fraction from heart sounds.
